## Deterministic policy

- Lights last lightsDurationMs (45000 by default); one visible local crew contribution at the Workshop relay restores them immediately. systems.lights.active is the actual authoritative visibility flag. Spatial/Core must use beaconRules.lightsVisionRadius for crew while active and saboteurVisionRadius for saboteurs. The module does not replace spatial projection.
- Communications last communicationsDurationMs (35000); one visible local crew contribution at the Bridge radio restores them immediately. systems.communications.active blocks devices; the safe projection also reports devicesBlocked. Investigation must cancel/refuse reads while this flag is active.
- Doors close for doorDurationMs (12000), reopen automatically at their original deadline and reopen on every non-roam phase. Any living, non-transit keeper can physically reopen a nearby visible door from either outside face; no role is required for physical opening. Repair panel points lie bodyRadius+4 beyond the collider, so reopening does not require seeing through a closed door. Core must use closed door rectangles for collision/LOS and handle an actor already overlapping a just-closed collider without permanently trapping it. There are at most two simultaneously closed doors by default, with a fixed absolute bound of the public door count.
- One lights disruption, one communications disruption and bounded doors may coexist with one critical. Auxiliary sabotage shares one per-actor20000ms cooldown; it is independent of critical sabotage. Defaults may be overridden by coordinator-owned beaconRules.auxiliaryCooldownMs and maxClosedDoors (1..public door count). These shared additions were requested; absent keys use the documented module constants. Other duration keys come from shared DEFAULT_BEACON_RULES.
- Power and flood have distinct public two-site fixture sets. Canonical sabotage preserves active,kind,roomId,deadlineAt,repairsCompleted,repairsRequired,repairers and adds startedAt/sites. roomId is the first site's room for legacy compatibility. Each site needs one instantaneous accepted contribution (no held repair timer), two DIFFERENT living crew are required across the two sites, and one keeper cannot contribute twice. Progress is public; contributor identities remain private. Completed critical state is retained with active:false. Critical duration/cooldown use existing rules.criticalDurationMs (75000) and sabotageCooldownMs (65000); initialization honors each actor's existing sabotageAt, and a critical updates that legacy field too. Legacy sabotage maps to power; legacy repair selects only an available visible nearby current-critical site.
- At exactly a critical deadline, repair/system actions reject. The coordinator alone ends the match, with critical failure before maximum/phase/noncritical changes at a tie. systemsDeadline intentionally excludes critical failure. Reports/council do not pause lights/comms or critical clocks. End/pause clears auxiliary effects and all doors/transit; it does not invent an outcome or replace the canonical critical record.

## Secret hatch travel and evidence

Public fixtures are validated for bounded unique IDs, real walkable room positions, distinct reciprocal links and no dangling/self links. Travel takes ventTravelMs (1600); movement is stopped and private systems.transit[actor] retains origin and entry/exit/deadline. Core must hide this actor from spatial interactions/projection while in transit and deny movement/interactions. A validated arrival alone moves the actor to the exit fixture. Cooldown is charged at entry through scheduled arrival+ventCooldownMs (15000), with no refund after cancellation. Council/non-roam or death cancels in place, with no destination teleport.

No vent identity/location is sent through publicEvent. Private replay records vent_enter and vent_exit carry explicit visibleTo containing the actor plus living non-transit observers who can actually see BOTH actor and local hatch at that moment. Entry witnesses do not learn exitId; exit witnesses do not learn entryId. vent_cancelled is actor-only. Coordinator/World may project an entitled record as local evidence but must remove private visibleTo recipient lists from client envelopes; never publish the raw record globally. Raw fully attributed action/replay remains ended-only. getSystemsView exposes selfTransit and cooldowns only for the requested actor, never a transit map, other cooldowns, origin coordinates or repairer IDs.
